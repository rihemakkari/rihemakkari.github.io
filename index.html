Case files. Each one states the problem before the solution.

File 01 · 2026

DarkWatch: dark-web threat intelligence platform (ANCS)

Problem
Ransomware claims and credential leaks about national organisations surface on dark-web forums and leak sites, scattered and hard to track systematically.
What I built
A pipeline that collects from 14 sources, deduplicates findings, classifies them into 6 threat categories with an NLP model, and triages them by severity, with a SOC integration prepared via webhook.
Stack
  • Python
  • scikit-learn (TF-IDF + logistic regression)
  • Security Onion
Result
108 unique findings (6 critical, 35 high) from 127 collected; 82.6% classifier accuracy; supervisor evaluation 19/20.

Writing & research

A working paper, essays and a workshop.

  1. 2026 Working paper

    DARKWATCH: A Threat Intelligence Architecture for Dark Web Monitoring and Its Implications for Geopolitical Power Asymmetries

    Unpublished · Available on request

    Presents DarkWatch's NLP architecture for dark-web monitoring and argues that threat intelligence is geopolitical infrastructure, in which the Global South is mostly observed rather than observing.

  2. Dec 2025 Essay

    We All Look the Same Now: Notes on the Algorithmic Production of Sameness

    Medium

    A Barthesian reading of platform culture: feeds reward legibility over difference, so identity becomes a template we select rather than invent.

  3. Dec 2025 Essay

    Digital Fortresses and Invisible Chains: A Political Anatomy of Cybersecurity

    Medium

    Written from inside the profession, it asks whom security actually protects, from cloud platforms to biometric borders, and sketches a "radical security" grounded in collective care.
